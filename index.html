🚀 Create Your Free Server

Follow these steps to get your free Minecraft server. Your site stays open the whole time!

  1. Click "Open Aternos" below — it opens in a new tab
  2. Create a free Aternos account (takes 1 minute)
  3. Click "Create server" and pick a name
  4. Copy your server IP (looks like yourname.aternos.me)
  5. Come back here and paste it below!
